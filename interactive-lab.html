02 / notation

The same structure appears in my work

A cube has a state, legal moves, constraints, and a measurable solved state. My work keeps returning to the same pattern: observe a system, choose a move, measure the result.

Cube → research

State becomes a time series, network trace, or traffic queue. Move becomes a policy, remediation, or model choice. Result becomes error, risk, latency, or clearance time.
